Semi-automated offside

Semi-automated offside technology uses tracking cameras, artificial intelligence, and ball data to monitor players’ positions in real time. When a pass is made, the system automatically identifies possible offside situations and sends an alert to the VAR team, which checks the play before informing the referee. The technology is already officially used in major competitions.

-

Tie-breaker

Replay analysis is a traditional technological resource used to analyze and illustrate football plays with greater precision. Through images and virtualizations, it allows situations such as offside decisions, goals, and the distance or speed of the ball to be analyzed. Nowadays, its use by commentators has decreased due to the development of other analysis tools, and it is mainly used as an illustrative resource in broadcasts.

-

GPS vest

The GPS vest is a device used to monitor athletes’ performance during training sessions and competitions. Through sensors, it records data such as distance covered, speed, acceleration, and positioning, assisting in performance analysis and training planning. The technology is already widely used in professional football.
