Dynamic LED Courts

Dynamic LED courts are sports surfaces that use LED panels integrated into the floor to display game lines, animations, statistics, and other visual elements. The markings can be changed digitally, allowing the court to be adapted for different sports. This technology has already been officially used in basketball competitions, but it is not yet common and is being gradually adopted at sporting events.

-

Airless Gen1

The Airless Gen1 is a basketball developed by Wilson after six years of research and development. Its 3D-printed internal structure allows the ball to maintain its shape and performance without the need for air. The project was developed and tested at W Labs using advanced engineering software. Although it is commercially available, the Airless Gen1 is not officially used in NBA games or professional competitions.

-

CBB Radar

CBB Radar is a platform for analyzing and monitoring Brazilian basketball players. The system gathers information from different sources and allows users to track performance data such as efficiency, playing volume, shooting profile, and defensive performance. The platform is mainly designed for athlete analysis and monitoring and is not a technology officially used to control or officiate games.
